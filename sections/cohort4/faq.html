Before You Decide

Still Have Questions?

Here are some of the questions beginners ask before joining.

I don't have a laptop. Can I still join?

Yes. Most of the skills can be learned using your smartphone. Virtual Assistance may require a laptop for some advanced features.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Absolutely. The Academy is designed for beginners, so you don't need previous digital skills experience.

I can't afford ₦25,000 at once. What can I do?

You can secure your seat with ₦5,000 and complete the remaining balance before registration closes. The installment option is ₦28,000 in total.

I'm too busy. What if I miss a class?

Classes are recorded, so you can watch them later. You can learn around your existing schedule while still completing the training.

Will I get a job after the training?

No training can guarantee a job. What the Academy does is equip you with practical skills, a portfolio, personal branding, and client acquisition strategies to help you pursue opportunities.

When does registration close?

Registration for Cohort 4 closes on September 19th. This is the last cohort of the year.
